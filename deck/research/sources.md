## Community advice (Reddit/Medium)
- r/EngineeringStudents, r/BTECH_students threads and Medium SIH-prep articles:
  solve from the user's pain, not the tech; the working demo outweighs a polished
  deck; don't over-design (the official template is plain — clarity wins); state
  beneficiary and route to them; common misses: too much text, reading slides aloud,
  omitting PS number/theme, skipping the "resources required" slide.
- Official template & format: sih.gov.in → Resources.
